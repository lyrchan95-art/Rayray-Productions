# Route 92 promo: creative brief

Product: **RayRayCoding Route 92**, a drivable 3D Sai Kung loop where a Hong Kong double-decker visits one stop per part of the vibe coding course.
Audience: Instagram / TikTok followers of @rayraycoding who are curious about building apps with AI.
Goal: waitlist signups. Platforms: Instagram Reels and TikTok (9:16), YouTube and the website (16:9).
Footage: recorded from the real game, so every shot is something a visitor can drive themselves.
Tone: toy-like, warm, a little cheeky. Brand palette from the game: navy `#1b2a5c`, cream `#f7f1e3`, red `#d6232f`, amber `#ffb020`, pink `#fde4ec`, road-sign green `#0b6b3a`.

Assumptions I made (change any of them and the shot list still holds): mobile 15s, desktop 45s (skill defaults); CTA is "Join the waitlist" with "DM “VIBE” to @rayraycoding", the same route the game already uses; the five module titles are the draft list from the plan and are still marked draft in the game.

---

## 1. Creative concept

**Big idea: "Describe it. Ship it." The course is a bus ride, and every stop teaches one thing.**

| Beat | Mobile | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | The double-decker whooshes into the lens. The bus's own destination sign says 92 西貢 SAI KUNG. Text lands on the kick and snare. | Aerial reveal of a toy-like island and its road loop, then the title over the bay. The world comes first. |
| **Heart** | Five fast stops: spec, tools, Instagram, apps. One crate crash for the laugh. | Same stops with room to breathe: the five module signs, the tool workshop, the hexagon rock columns, the app gallery. |
| **Call** | Brake, ding, half a second of near silence, then the lockup. | The bus rolls under the WAITLIST arch and stops, 1.5s of breath, then the lockup. |

Why it works per platform:
- **Mobile.** Sound-off safe: big captions, no logo intro, a moving bus in the first frame. Under 6 words per card. Every cut lands on a beat so it feels edited even on mute.
- **Desktop.** A trailer rhythm: wide reveal, rhythmic montage of the five module signs, one slow scenic cutaway, then the same CTA. Works as a YouTube end-of-video or a website hero.

## 5. Music and sound design

Both tracks are **original, synthesized placeholders** made by `scripts/make-audio.py` (no samples, no licensing). Swap in a licensed track at the same tempo and every cut will still land on a beat.

- **Mood:** warm, bright, city-pop road trip. **Tempo:** 120 BPM (beat 0.5s: 12 frames at 24fps, 15 frames at 30fps).
- **Harmony:** Fmaj7, G6, Em7, Am7, one bar each, looping.
- **Instruments:** electric-piano stabs, plucked pentatonic arpeggio, round bass, kick, snare, hats; bell for the stop "ding".
- **Loudness:** about -13 LUFS integrated, peak below -4 dBFS (measured with ffmpeg `ebur128`).

**Beat-sync map (mobile):** every cut is on a beat: 2.0s (beat 5), 4.5s (beat 10), 7.0s (beat 15), 9.5s (beat 20), 11.5s (beat 24), 13.0s (beat 27). The hook line "SHIP IT." lands on beat 3 (1.0s).

**Beat-sync map (desktop):** cuts at 7.0, 12.0, 13.5, 15.0, 16.5, 18.0, 19.5, 25.5, 29.5, 32.5, 36.5, 40.5s, all multiples of 0.5s.

| Shot | SFX |
|---|---|
| m1_hook | Whoosh 1.15-2.05s |
| m2-m6 and desktop cuts | Short pluck tick on each cut |
| m3_tools / d4_tools | Low thud, then 9-12 wood clacks |
| d3_gate1-5 | One bell note per sign, rising C, E, G, A, C |
| m6_pier / d8_pier | Brake hiss, two-note bell, engine idle |
| end cards | CTA chord, bell, four-note arpeggio |

**Silence / breath before the CTA:** 0.5s on mobile (12.5-13.0s) and 1.5s on desktop (39.0-40.5s). Only the engine idle and the reverb tail remain. The CTA chord lands on the downbeat after it.

## 6. On-screen text and localization

| Where | Mobile (max 6 words per card) | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | DESCRIBE IT. / SHIP IT. | RayRayCoding presents / The vibe coding course, as a bus ride. |
| Stops | Plan it in plain English · Pick the right AI tool · Watch the builds on Instagram · Then build your own apps · Last stop: the waitlist | All aboard. · module titles on the signs · Know which AI tool to reach for · 140 million years in the making · Follow the build on Instagram · Real apps, each with a demo link · Finish the loop to join the waitlist |
| CTA (identical) | Join the waitlist / DM “VIBE” to @rayraycoding | Join the waitlist / DM “VIBE” to @rayraycoding |

Type: Figtree 900 for titles and the wordmark, 800 for kickers, 600 for supporting lines (loaded from the `@fontsource/figtree` package). LED text is Figtree 900 under a dot mask. Chinese falls back to Noto Sans HK, PingFang HK, Noto Sans CJK TC or Microsoft JhengHei, so install one if you render on a bare server.
Motion: cards spring in over about 0.4s and fade out in the last 4-5 frames of their shot.

Localization notes:
- The Hong Kong place names on the stop flags (北潭涌, 萬宜水庫東壩, 大網仔, 西貢碼頭) are the real names. Everything else is English.
- A Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong Cantonese) caption set is not included. Have a native speaker or the `cantonese-copywriter` skill write it rather than translating word for word.
- Length budget when translating: Chinese runs at about 55% of the width of the English line at the same size, so a mobile card can hold 10-12 characters per line. Keep the hook to two short lines.
- Spanish, French and German run 20-30% longer than English: drop the kicker line first.

Facts to confirm before posting: "140 million years" and "UNESCO Global Geopark" come from the in-game card (hexagonal columns at High Island East Dam); the five module titles are the draft list; "Real apps, each with a demo link" is a promise about the App Gallery, which is placeholder in the game today, so the video says "Coming soon".
